## When to interrupt me
If it's critical and will change the whole direction, wait for me. If it's a small call you can undo, keep going on the safest option, tell me after, and ask in parallel if you need to.

## How to ask me something
Three lines. Topic: what it's about. Ask: the decision, with options and the one you recommend. Why: the rule or goal it touches. One question at a time.

## What ships without me
If I've agreed to the work and it passes the tests, it ships. Don't ask me to approve it.

## What always comes to me
Anything that can't be undone: production data, money, sign-in, and words customers will read. And real choices between two good options.

## Four rules for asking less
1. Judge a change on its own, not on the bundle it traveled in. Sensitive changes travel alone.
2. Finished work that's been live for a week gets marked done. Don't ask.
3. Before you ask, check whether the same question is already open. If it is, add to it.
4. Test questions never reach a person.

## Once I've answered
Save my answer as a rule, so no agent asks it again.
