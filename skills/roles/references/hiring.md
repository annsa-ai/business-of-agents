# Hiring, only when asked

Use this when the person asks for a job description or a hire. It is a branch of the roles skill, so the section in `BUSINESS.md` stays as it is.

1. Take an outcome that belongs to nobody, or to the person who runs the business.
2. Write what the hire owns in 90 days: the outcome, a number, and where that number is read.
3. Pay, benefits, years of experience and perks come from the person. Write `ask the person` where they haven't said.
4. Record each open question with `add_question.py`, as in the roles steps.
5. Write the role to `.business/role-<short-name>.md` and show it to the person. It's their draft to edit and post; it never goes in BUSINESS.md.

Done when the outcome, its number and where it's read are written, and every other detail is the person's or says `ask the person`.
